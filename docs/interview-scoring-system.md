# Scoring System for Each Response

- **0 - Incorrect**  
  The response is completely wrong, irrelevant, or shows a fundamental misunderstanding of the question.

- **1 - Incorrect with Positive Elements**  
  The response is mostly incorrect but includes some elements that demonstrate a partial understanding of the topic or a minor correct aspect.

- **2 - Basic / Borderline Good**  
  The response addresses the question at a very basic level, showing minimal understanding. It might have some correct points but lacks depth and completeness.

- **3 - Correct with Room for Improvement**  
  The response correctly answers the question and demonstrates a good understanding, but there are areas that could be improved. The answer is sufficient to be considered correct but could be more comprehensive or detailed.

- **4 - Excellent / Perfect Response**  
  The response is thorough, well-structured, and demonstrates an excellent understanding of the topic. It is complete, with no significant errors or omissions, and exceeds expectations.

# Scoring System for Calculating Overall Score

### Overall Average Score Scale

- **0.0 - 0.5: Poor**  
  The overall responses indicate a fundamental lack of understanding or incorrect answers.

- **0.6 - 1.5: Below Average**  
  The responses are mostly incorrect but show some elements of understanding.

- **1.6 - 2.5: Basic**  
  The responses are basic and show minimal understanding, with some correct points but lacking depth.

- **2.6 - 3.5: Good**  
  The responses are mostly correct and demonstrate a good understanding, though there are areas for improvement.

- **3.6 - 4.0: Excellent**  
  The responses are thorough, well-structured, and demonstrate excellent understanding, exceeding expectations.
