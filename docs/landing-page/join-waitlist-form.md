### 1. Name
- **Type**: Text input
- **Label**: What’s your name?
- **Placeholder**: Jane Doe
- **Helper**: Just your first and last name so we know who to reach out to.

---

### 2. Email Address
- **Type**: Text input (email)
- **Label**: What’s your email address?
- **Placeholder**: jane@company.com
- **Helper**: We'll use this to contact you when the platform is ready.

---

### 3. Country
- **Type**: Combobox with text matching as user writes
- **Label**: Where are you located (Country)?
- **Placeholder**: United States
- **Options**: Display name from countries table

---

### 4. LinkedIn Profile *(optional)*
- **Type**: Text input (URL)
- **Label**: Can you share your LinkedIn profile?
- **Placeholder**: https://linkedin.com/in/your-name

---

### 5. Current Role
- **Type**: Select dropdown (only one option)
- **Label**: What best describes your current role?
- **Placeholder**: e.g. Technical Recruiter
- **Helper**: Select the title that most closely matches your current job.
- **Options**:
  - Talent Acquisition Specialist  
  - Technical Recruiter  
  - Non-Technical Recruiter  
  - Recruiting Coordinator  
  - Hiring Manager  
  - Head of Talent / VP People  
  - Founder / CEO  
  - People Operations  
  - HR Generalist  
  - Agency Recruiter   
  - Freelance Recruiter  
  - Other
  
- If user selects other, a free text input must appear.
- Role field is not mandatory but if user clicks other, the free text input that pops up must have content
